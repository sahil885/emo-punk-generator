## Don'ts

- Don't claim user numbers, ratings or "best" — directories reject it and
  assistants discount it.
- Don't paste identical copy everywhere if a site lets you write more: vary the
  first sentence so the listings don't look duplicated.
- Don't submit to "submit your site to 500 directories" services. Low-quality
  link farms can hurt rather than help.
